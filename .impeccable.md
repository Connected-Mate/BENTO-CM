# Design Context — Coding Mate training

## Users
French-speaking UI, UX and product designers who want to build with AI, plus company training buyers and training partners. Main task: understand the concrete outcomes of Vibe Designing, review the programme and contact Alexandre. Existing Connected Mate visitors must still find apps, talks, podcasts and services.

## Brand personality
Precise, empowering, reassuring. User explicitly requests a more technical, pleasant and trustworthy site. Show creative autonomy and production experience, with no unsupported promises.

## Aesthetic direction
Light editorial interface; clear large sans-serif headlines, IBM Plex Mono for functional labels, restrained green highlights from the actual site palette. Flat layouts, diagrams and examples over repeated boxed cards. No purple gradients, Inter, nested cards or invented social proof.

## Principles
1. Training first; clear learning outcomes before tool names.
2. Designers retain judgment; agents execute.
3. Explain developer integration and production review honestly.
4. Four days recommended, adaptable to the person or team.
5. Mobile and keyboard paths receive the same attention as desktop.
