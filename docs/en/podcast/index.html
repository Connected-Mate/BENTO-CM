The Connected Mate podcast

Connected Mate started here, in 2018: a live broadcast from the street, with a simple smartphone and listeners interacting in real time. More than 1,000 episodes later, the format has changed, but its purpose has not.
Message us on WhatsApp → (new tab)

What it is

The Connected Mate podcast is a series of conversations with the brightest minds in tech, innovation and business. Not a news format, not a weekly digest: one person, one subject, as long as it takes to get to the bottom of it.

The podcast exists in audio, and in video on YouTube. It is where Connected Mate began, before the training, the services and the apps, and it remains the thread that ties everything else together: much of what we say on stage was a conversation at the microphone first.

The podcast explores how generative and agentic AI are transforming business models, alongside innovation, entrepreneurship, digital transformation, privacy and the future of work. These are the same themes as our talks, but approached from the other side: here, the guests do the talking.
